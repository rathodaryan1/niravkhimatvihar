# Nirav Khimat Vihar --- Content Specification

## Brand

**Nirav Khimat Vihar**

Suggested positioning:

**A Peaceful Stay for Your Palitana Yatra**

## Content principles

-   Warm and respectful.
-   Accurate and factual.
-   Avoid unsupported claims.
-   Use real property photographs where possible.
-   Do not invent reviews.
-   Do not publish unverified pricing.
-   Do not publish unverified contact information.

## Required verified content

Management must provide/confirm:

-   Official property name
-   Official address
-   Contact numbers
-   WhatsApp number
-   Email
-   Room types
-   Physical room inventory
-   Capacity per room
-   Bed configuration
-   Room amenities
-   Prices
-   Taxes/service charges
-   Check-in time
-   Check-out time
-   Cancellation/refund rules
-   Eligibility requirements
-   ID requirements
-   Bhojanshala timings
-   Bhojanshala prices
-   Parking information
-   Driver accommodation rules
-   Gallery photos
-   Logo/brand assets
-   Official social links
